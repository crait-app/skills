WeRead

WeRead Assistant - Search books, manage bookshelf, view notes and highlights, browse reviews, reading statistics, discover recommended books. Supports Chinese and English.

Notes scattered everywhere, difficult to manage and export

Want to find similar books but recommendations are not accurate enough

Bookshelf is messy, hard to find specific books quickly

Reading progress lost, don't know where I left off
